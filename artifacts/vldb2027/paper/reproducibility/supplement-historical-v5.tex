\section{Historical Evidence and Auxiliary Cases}
\label{sec:historical-results}
The accepted offline Task A synthesis provides a training-regime contrast that the new frozen-model study deliberately does not repeat. It covers 24 checkpoints and 16 original fit plans per checkpoint, retaining all three seeds. For HyboNet/Airport, the full-trained four-corner accuracies (sampled, local, upstream, both) are 79.1141\%, 79.8407\%, 92.6383\% and 93.4174\%; the matched-trained values are 88.8480\%, 89.3557\%, 89.4914\% and 89.9860\%. Upstream restoration therefore changes from $+13.5242$ to $+0.6434$ points, while local restoration changes from $+0.7265$ to $+0.5077$. The checkpoints differ between training regimes. This is descriptive development evidence about different learned functions, not a same-weight causal training intervention or an independent final-test result.

On Disease-NC, HyboNet's full-trained four-corner means are 80.9524\%, 83.3829\%, 91.4187\% and 95.2381\%; matched means are 76.1905\%, 77.2321\%, 77.8770\% and 79.3651\%. The historical HGCN has only one message layer, so upstream restoration is a structural alias of sampled computation and joint restoration aliases local restoration. Its Disease-NC matched mean falls from 63.2937\% sampled to 62.6984\% under local restoration. Seed 11 has 11 corrections and 23 harms over 672 node--plan events, despite a positive mean-margin change; the degenerate seed 37 remains at 50\%. These cases are retained and do not describe the new three-layer HGCN.

\paragraph{Predictive explanation and correction remain bounded.}
Earlier held-out explanatory prediction tests obtained geometric/operator-feature cross-entropy reductions of 2.8669\% and 1.1289\%, below the registered 5\% criterion; margin-MSE and calibration criteria also failed. This study does not refit them. The HyboNet E1 correction scored 89.5658\%, compared with 89.5965\% for ordinary averaging of the same two native-logit views and 89.0275\% for one view. Among 143 differing predictions, E1 corrected 61 and harmed 68; a single-view gain did not establish incremental value over the equal-information control.

A separate HGCN/arxiv correction confirmation gives K15 means of 71.2164\%, 71.2880\% and 71.2220\% for plain, candidate and generic control, respectively. Candidate changes across seeds are $-0.0906$, $+0.1292$ and $+0.1762$ points, while request time rises from 118.174 to 155.623 ms and allocated memory from 303 to 1,019 MiB. These adapted weights are not substituted into the new original-plain comparison. Conditional positive WordNet results belong to a different operator/task/development contract and do not reverse these limitations. Complete historical tables, plots, controls, calibration failures and source references are retained in the indexed companion historical files, including the original SAGE gate and exact/RAM records; no historical sign or seed is removed to support the new narrative.
